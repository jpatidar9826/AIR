# Interview Panel Guidelines

## Panel composition
Each interview panel should have two to three members. At least one panelist must have hands-on expertise in the core skills of the role, and at least one must be senior to the level being hired. Where possible, panels should include members from different teams and backgrounds to reduce individual bias.

## Interviewer eligibility
Interviewers must complete structured interviewing and unconscious bias training before joining a panel. An interviewer should not conduct more than five interviews per week so that each candidate gets proper attention.

## Conflicts of interest
A panelist who knows the candidate personally, has worked closely with them, or is related to them must declare this and step aside from the panel.

## Structured interviews
All candidates for the same role are asked the same core questions, derived from the job description. Panelists may ask follow-up questions to explore answers. Questions about age, religion, family plans, health, nationality or other personal matters unrelated to the job are not permitted.

## Scoring
Each panelist scores the candidate independently on a 1 to 5 scale for every competency before discussing with other panelists. Scores and written evidence are submitted within 24 hours of the interview.

## Feedback to candidates
Candidates who are not selected after an interview receive a response within ten working days. Feedback, when requested, must be based on the documented scores and evidence.
