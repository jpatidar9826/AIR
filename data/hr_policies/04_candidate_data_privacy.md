# Candidate Data Privacy Policy

## Data collected
We collect only the candidate information needed to assess suitability for a role: contact details, resume, qualifications, work history, assessment results and interview notes.

## Consent and purpose
Candidate data is used only for recruitment. Candidates are told how their data will be used when they apply. Data will not be used for other purposes or shared outside the hiring team without consent.

## Use of external tools
Candidate resumes and personal data must not be uploaded to unapproved online tools or public AI services. Approved AI services may be used only according to the company's AI usage guidelines, and personal identifiers such as email addresses and phone numbers should be masked before processing where possible.

## Retention
Data for unsuccessful candidates is kept for twelve months and then deleted, unless the candidate agrees to be considered for future roles. Data for hired candidates moves to the employee record.

## Candidate rights
Candidates can ask to see, correct or delete their personal data by contacting HR. Requests are answered within thirty days.

## Security
Recruitment data is stored only in approved systems with access limited to the recruiting team and the interview panel for that role.
