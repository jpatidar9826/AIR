# Offer and Onboarding Policy

## Offer approval
Offers are prepared by HR after the hiring decision is approved. Salary must fall within the approved band for the grade. Any offer above the band requires approval from the department head and the HR business partner.

## Background verification
Offers are conditional on satisfactory background verification, including education, previous employment and identity checks. Verification is completed by an approved agency within fifteen working days.

## Offer validity
An offer letter is valid for seven calendar days unless extended by HR. Candidates may negotiate the joining date, which normally should be within ninety days of the offer.

## Notice and joining
The hiring manager prepares a 30-60-90 day onboarding plan before the joining date. On day one, new joiners complete induction, receive equipment and are assigned a buddy for their first three months.

## Probation
New employees serve a probation period of six months. A probation review is held at the end of the period to confirm employment.
