# Recruitment and Selection Policy

## Purpose
This policy sets out how open positions are advertised, how candidates are screened, and how hiring decisions are made. The goal is to hire the best qualified person for each role through a fair, consistent and documented process.

## Requisition and approval
A hiring manager must raise a job requisition and obtain approval from the department head and HR before a position is advertised. The requisition must include the job title, grade, required skills, minimum qualifications, experience range and budget.

## Job descriptions
Every vacancy must have an approved job description. Requirements listed as mandatory must be genuinely necessary to perform the job. Job descriptions must use inclusive, gender-neutral language and must not include requirements related to age, marital status or other protected characteristics.

## Screening
Resumes are screened against the mandatory requirements of the job description. Automated or AI-assisted screening tools may be used to rank candidates, but a recruiter must review every AI recommendation before a candidate is rejected. No candidate may be rejected solely by an automated system.

## Shortlisting
A shortlist normally contains three to six candidates per vacancy. The recruiter documents the reason each candidate was shortlisted or not shortlisted, based only on job-related criteria such as skills, experience and qualifications.

## Assessment
Shortlisted candidates complete at least one structured interview. Technical roles may also include a skills test or case study. The same assessment must be used for all candidates applying for the same vacancy.

## Hiring decision
The interview panel recommends a candidate with written justification. The hiring manager and HR jointly approve the final decision. Records of the selection process are kept for twelve months.
