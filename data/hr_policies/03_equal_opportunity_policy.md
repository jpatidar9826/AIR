# Equal Opportunity and Anti-Discrimination Policy

## Commitment
The company provides equal employment opportunity to all applicants and employees. Decisions about recruitment, selection, promotion and pay are based only on merit, qualifications and job requirements.

## Protected characteristics
Discrimination on the basis of age, gender, gender identity, race, ethnicity, religion, caste, disability, marital status, pregnancy, sexual orientation or nationality is prohibited at every stage of hiring.

## Use of AI in hiring
AI tools used in recruitment must not use protected characteristics as inputs. Models must be tested for adverse impact before use and at least every six months. If the selection rate for any group is less than 80 percent of the rate for the highest group, HR must review the tool and the process before continuing.

## Reasonable adjustments
Candidates with a disability may request reasonable adjustments for interviews or assessments, such as extra time, accessible venues or alternative formats. Requests are handled confidentially by HR.

## Raising concerns
Candidates or employees who believe they have been treated unfairly can raise a concern with HR or through the ethics helpline. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is prohibited.
